# For grant applicants: Multimodal AI for Inclusive Grant Applications

An AI-powered grant application system that helps small business owners create complete applications from **voice input, business documents, and photos**. It supports **English, Amharic, and Afaan Oromo**, using speech-to-text, OCR, computer vision, translation, and LLMs to extract and structure information into **validated, editable applications** that can also be automatically evaluated and ranked.

# For grant providers: AI-Powered Grant Screening & Evaluation Platform
An AI-powered platform that helps grant providers review, evaluate, and prioritize applications at scale. It automatically assesses applications against predefined eligibility requirements and scoring criteria, verifies consistency across business licenses, application information, and photos, identifies missing information and potential risks, and generates transparent scores, recommendations, and rankings for human reviewers.
